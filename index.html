About GIMR

Godavari Institute of Management and Research (GIMR) is a private management college in Jalgaon, Maharashtra, established in 2001 under the aegis of the Godavari Foundation.


The institute offers management education with a focus on developing professional knowledge, skills, and leadership capabilities.
